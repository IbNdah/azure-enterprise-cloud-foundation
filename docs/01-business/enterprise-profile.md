# Executive Summary

Mandara Global is a fictional multinational enterprise created for educational and portfolio purposes. It is not affiliated with any existing company or organization.

Mandara Global is a global industrial technology company operating across manufacturing, energy, logistics, and digital services. With operations spanning multiple continents and thousands of employees, the company delivers innovative products and services to customers worldwide.

To support its long-term business strategy, Mandara Global has launched **OneCloud 2030**, a strategic digital transformation program aimed at modernizing its global IT landscape through cloud technologies, standardized governance, and platform engineering.

This document provides an overview of the organization, its business model, strategic objectives, and the challenges that drive its cloud transformation journey.

# Business Challenges

Over the years, each Business Unit has independently adopted technologies and operational processes.

This decentralized approach has created several challenges:

- inconsistent cloud governance
- duplicated infrastructure
- different security standards
- limited visibility of cloud costs
- slow provisioning of new environments
- operational complexity across regions

These challenges have become a barrier to the company's digital transformation ambitions.

---

# Strategic Priorities

Mandara Global has identified the following strategic priorities for the coming years:

- accelerate innovation
- simplify global operations
- strengthen cybersecurity
- standardize cloud adoption
- improve cost transparency
- increase deployment automation
- enable AI-driven business capabilities

These priorities directly support the OneCloud 2030 transformation program.

# Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE)

To lead its cloud transformation, Mandara Global established a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE).

The CCoE defines enterprise standards for:

- Cloud Governance
- Security
- Identity
- Networking
- Platform Engineering
- Infrastructure as Code
- DevSecOps
- FinOps
- Architecture Reviews

The CCoE works closely with all Business Units to ensure consistent cloud adoption across the organization.

---

# Enterprise Vision

Mandara Global envisions a secure, governed, scalable, and AI-ready cloud platform that enables every Business Unit to innovate faster while maintaining enterprise-wide standards.

The cloud platform will become the digital foundation supporting future business growth and technological innovation.
